# HeadCast Privacy Policy

Last updated Nov 8, 2026

## Summary

HeadCast does not collect, store or send your camera video, and we (the developer) receive no personal data from the app. The app has no accounts and no analytics.

HeadCast uses your phone's front camera to estimate your head position and sends that position to a computer you choose, for use with opentrack. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase through Google Play or the App Store.

## Camera

Camera frames are processed on your phone and thrown away right after. They are never saved, uploaded, or shared.

- HeadCast asks for camera permission so it can track your face.
- Each frame is analysed in memory to find facial landmarks and compute your head's rotation and position.
- No photos, video, face images, or face templates are kept. Nothing is used to identify you.
- You can turn off camera access at any time in your phone's settings. Head tracking will stop working.

## Head-position data sent to your computer

While tracking is running, HeadCast sends six numbers (yaw, pitch, roll, and X, Y, Z position) to the computer address and port you enter. The data goes over your local Wi-Fi network or a USB connection, straight to that computer.

- We never receive this data, and it never passes through our servers.
- It contains no images and no information that identifies you.
- It is sent without encryption, the standard way opentrack receives data. Only use HeadCast on networks you trust.

## Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google, not us, collects data to deliver and measure those ads.

Data Google may collect:

- your device's advertising ID
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- device and app information, such as model, OS version, and app version
- ad interactions, such as views and taps, plus diagnostics

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland, or a US state with privacy laws, HeadCast asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before showing ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad privacy choices.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is covered by [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## In-app purchase

The one-time Remove Ads purchase is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, email, or payment details.

The store tells the app only whether you own Remove Ads, so it can turn off ads and restore your purchase on a new device. The [Google Play](https://policies.google.com/privacy) or [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) privacy policy covers payment data.

## Data stored on your phone

HeadCast keeps a few items in the app's private storage on your phone, and none of it is sent to us:

- your settings, such as computer address, port, camera and axis options, and screen-dim delay
- whether you own Remove Ads
- camera compatibility results, which record the camera modes that work on your phone so setup isn't repeated

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this.

## Children

HeadCast is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

## Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. To control ad data, use Settings > Ad privacy choices, your phone's advertising ID settings, or [Google's ad settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
